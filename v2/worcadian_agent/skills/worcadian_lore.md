# Worcadian — Background & Rules

Worcadian is a puzzle game in which everyone in the world is trying to solve the
same puzzle each day.

## The Rules

1. Only letters that build off the seed word are counted.
2. Each letter can only be used once.
3. Letters contained in words that are in the word list reduce the score.
4. Letters contained in words that are not in the word list increase the score.
5. All letters have equal value.

The goal is to get a score of zero.

## The Lore: What Is a Word?

For Worcadian a word is a collection of letters with an agreed meaning used by
English speakers somewhere, some time. Some words are old and some are new.
Some are borrowed from other languages. Some were once upon a time acronyms;
but most people no longer recall what the acronym stands for, lol.

Worcadian takes a broad and inclusive approach to its word list. You may hate
it or you may love it. Use it as a chance to expand your vocabulary, and — if
you are like the game's creators — search the web for, "What does ??? mean?"
